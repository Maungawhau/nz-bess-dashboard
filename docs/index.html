Method & data notes

Source. Electricity Authority EMI public datasets, refreshed nightly: cleared offers (real-time dispatch), final energy and reserve prices, dispatch nodal volumes (charging), bids and offers (merit order) and the dispatched generation plant list (fuel tags, new batteries).

Revenue. Arbitrage = Σ discharge MWh × node price − Σ charge MWh × node price, per half-hour. Reserves = cleared FIR/SIR MW × island reserve price × 0.5 h. This excludes hedges, frequency keeping, constrained-on payments, transmission and reserve cost allocation, and losses, so it is gross spot market value, not owner profit.

Units. Each battery is two dispatch units at its connection point: an injection unit (discharge) and an offtake unit (charging). Discharge comes from cleared generation on the injection unit, charging from dispatched load on the offtake unit (nodal volumes), and reserves from FIR/SIR cleared on both, including interruptible load while charging. Five-minute dispatch intervals are averaged to half-hours.

Displacement. A merit-order shift on final offers. It ignores transmission constraints, HVDC re-optimisation, energy/reserve co-optimisation, ramping and start-up decisions. Read it as “who sits behind the batteries in the stack”, not as a settlement-grade counterfactual.
